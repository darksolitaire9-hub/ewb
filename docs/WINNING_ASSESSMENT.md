**If Demo Fails:**
- Have backup: Screenshots, video recording, or slides
- Acknowledge: "Technical difficulties happen - let me show you screenshots"
- Pivot: Focus on concept and evidence, not just demo

**If Judges Seem Skeptical:**
- Lean into evidence: "I understand the skepticism - that's why we have 28 peer-reviewed citations"

**If Time Runs Short:**
- Prioritize: Problem → Solution → Evidence → Business
