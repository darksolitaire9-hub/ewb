## ⚠️ Known Issues & Corrections

### Claims That Need Revision

**Issue 1: "23-31% improvement in emotional awareness"**
- **Problem:** Conflates engagement rates with clinical outcomes
- **Correction:** "23% higher engagement rates" (Bakker 2016)
- **Impact:** Moderate - claim is overstated but based on real data

**Issue 2: "5-7 days earlier detection" & "78-84% accuracy"**
- **Problem:** Specific statistics not clearly sourced in cited papers
- **Correction:** Need better citation or should be removed
- **Impact:** Low - these are secondary claims, not core evidence

**Issue 3: "3.2x faster pattern identification"**
- **Problem:** Specific multiplier not in cited paper
- **Correction:** "More efficient pattern identification" (qualitative claim)
- **Impact:** Low - supporting claim, not primary evidence

**Issue 4: Hass-Cohen & Carr (2008) - "31% enhancement"**
- **Problem:** This is a 2-page editorial, not an empirical study
- **Correction:** Should not be used to support specific percentage claims
- **Impact:** Moderate - weakens the "23-31%" range claim
