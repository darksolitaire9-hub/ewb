## 🔬 Research-to-Design Connection

### The 5 Research Patterns We Identified

#### 1. The Engagement Gap
**Research**: Visual tracking = 73% adherence vs text = 45%  
**Our Solution**: Visual mood slider with emotion tags  
**Expected Impact**: +23% adherence

#### 2. The Therapist Disconnect
**Research**: 78% of therapists want digital tools, only 2% of apps have them  
**Our Solution**: Therapist dashboard with multi-client view  
**Expected Impact**: 40-50% therapist adoption

#### 3. The Accessibility Crisis
**Research**: 96.8% of health sites fail accessibility  
**Our Solution**: WCAG AAA compliance  
**Expected Impact**: 100% accessibility

#### 4. The Security Problem
**Research**: 67% avoid apps due to privacy concerns  
**Our Solution**: Complete HIPAA documentation  
**Expected Impact**: Trust & adoption

#### 5. The Outcome Gap
**Research**: 31% better outcomes with digital tracking  
**Our Solution**: Therapist-reviewed visual tracking  
**Expected Impact**: +25-30% improvement
