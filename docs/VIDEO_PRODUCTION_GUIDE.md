# Video Production Guide
## AI-Generated Demo Videos for MoodBoard Pro Hackathon Submission

**Purpose:** Complete scripts and production guides for creating demo videos using AI tools (Runway, Synthesia, D-ID, etc.)  
**Target:** 3-5 minute demo video + 1-minute teaser  
**Showcase:** Bob's AI-powered development capabilities  
**Last Updated:** May 16, 2026

## 🎬 Video 1: Main Demo Video (3-5 minutes)

### Video Specifications
- **Duration:** 3-5 minutes
- **Format:** 1920x1080 (16:9) or 1080x1920 (9:16 for social)
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps
- **Audio:** Voiceover + background music (royalty-free)
- **Style:** Professional, clean, modern

### AI Tools Recommended
- **Voiceover:** ElevenLabs, Murf.ai, or Synthesia
- **Video Generation:** Runway ML, Synthesia, D-ID
- **Screen Recording:** OBS Studio, Loom, or ScreenFlow
- **Editing:** Descript (AI-powered), CapCut, or DaVinci Resolve
- **Music:** Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or AI-generated (Soundraw, AIVA)

**Duration:** 75 seconds

**Visual:**
- Screen recording of actual app (Client View)
- Cursor movements showing interaction
- Highlight key features with animated callouts

**Voiceover Script:**
```
"Let me show you how it works. Meet Sarah, a client tracking her mood.

[Show mood slider]

[Show emotion tags]
She selects emotions - happy, excited, and slightly anxious.

[Show notes field]

[Show submit button]

[Show AI insights]
Our AI instantly analyzes her entry, detecting patterns and providing personalized insights.

[Show insight card]
'Pattern detected: Your mood improves significantly on days you exercise. 
Recommendation: Consider morning exercise as part of your routine.'

[Show mood history chart]
Sarah can view her 7-day mood trends, seeing her progress visually.

This isn't just data collection - it's intelligent mood analysis that helps Sarah 
understand her emotional patterns."
```

**Screen Recording Instructions:**
1. Start on Client View
2. Slowly move slider from 5 to 7
3. Click emotion tags: Happy, Excited, Anxious
4. Type notes (use typing animation)
5. Click "Save Mood Entry"
6. Show success message
7. Scroll to AI insights section
8. Highlight key insights with animated box
9. Scroll to mood chart
10. Hover over data points

**Callout Annotations:**
- "Intuitive 1-10 Scale" (arrow to slider)
- "12 Emotion Tags" (circle around tags)
- "AI-Powered Insights" (highlight box)
- "7-Day Trend Visualization" (arrow to chart)

## 🎙️ Voiceover Production Guide

### Voice Characteristics
- **Tone:** Professional, confident, warm
- **Pace:** Moderate (140-160 words per minute)
- **Accent:** Neutral American or British English
- **Gender:** Your choice (both work well)
- **Age:** 30-45 years old sound

### AI Voice Generation Settings (ElevenLabs)
```
Voice: "Rachel" or "Adam" (professional, clear)
Stability: 0.75
Similarity: 0.85
Style Exaggeration: 0.25
Speaker Boost: Enabled
```

### Recording Tips
1. Add 0.5s pause after each sentence
2. Emphasize statistics (23%, 28%, 57.8M)
3. Slow down for key phrases ("Built with Bob", "28 citations")
4. Add slight enthusiasm for benefits
5. Professional but not robotic

## 🎨 Visual Effects & Editing

### Callout Animations
**Style:** Modern, clean, non-distracting

**Types:**
1. **Arrow Callouts:** Point to specific UI elements
2. **Circle Highlights:** Draw attention to areas
3. **Box Overlays:** Highlight sections
4. **Text Labels:** Explain features

**Animation:**
- Fade in: 0.3s
- Hold: 2-3s
- Fade out: 0.3s
- Easing: Ease-in-out

### Transitions
**Between Scenes:**
- Fade: 0.5s (most common)
- Wipe: 0.3s (for contrast)
- Zoom: 0.4s (for emphasis)

**Within Scenes:**
- Cut: For quick changes
- Dissolve: For smooth flow

### Text Overlays
**Statistics:**
- Font: Inter Bold or Montserrat Bold
- Size: 72pt
- Color: White with dark shadow
- Animation: Count up effect
- Duration: 2-3s on screen

**Captions:**
- Font: Inter Regular
- Size: 36pt
- Color: White with black background bar
- Position: Bottom third
- Always on for accessibility

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Make It Engaging
1. **Start with a hook:** Grab attention in first 3 seconds
2. **Show, don't tell:** Demo > talking heads
3. **Use real data:** Actual app interactions, not mockups
4. **Add personality:** "Built with Bob" is unique - emphasize it
5. **Keep it moving:** No scene longer than 30 seconds

### Technical Quality
1. **Audio is king:** Bad audio kills good video
2. **Lighting matters:** Even for screen recordings (webcam)
3. **Smooth movements:** Slow, deliberate cursor movements
4. **Clean interface:** Hide unnecessary UI elements
5. **Test everything:** Record a test run first

### Storytelling
1. **Problem → Solution:** Classic structure works
2. **Show transformation:** Before/after, pain/relief
3. **Use emotion:** Mental health is personal
4. **Build credibility:** Research citations matter
5. **End strong:** Clear CTA, memorable closing
